### Prepared English description

**Scavenge. Rebuild. Survive.**

BIOSO is a survival roguelite where your creature is your build. Explore a world where nature is reclaiming forgotten technology, collect parts, and combine bodies, weapons, legs, and internal modules to shape your own playstyle.

Your weapons attack automatically. Focus on movement, choose upgrades, and decide which parts are worth carrying into the next fight.

**Make every part count**

- Assemble your creature from interchangeable bodies, weapons, legs, and internal modules.
- Discover abilities and combine upgrades into new synergies.
- Survive enemy swarms, face elites, and challenge bosses.

**Controls**

- Move: WASD, arrow keys, or drag on the ground with your mouse or finger.
- Assembly and inventory: I or the on-screen Assembly button.
- Map: M or the on-screen Map button.
- Pause: Escape or the on-screen Pause button.
- Weapons attack automatically.

**Play in your browser**

The game uses a portrait layout on both mobile and desktop. English and Russian are available in Settings. BIOSO is in development; features and balance may change.

**Follow the development**

Enjoyed building your creature? Add BIOSO to a collection and follow kolyabeton for future updates. Feedback is welcome: which combination of parts worked best for you?

**AI disclosure**

BIOSO uses AI-assisted code and graphics alongside human direction, editing, and testing.
