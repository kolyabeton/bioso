## Provenance notes

- `Wasp Wings 2` is the real-insect basis for both previews.
- `Insectoid Monster Clicking` is not marked as AI-generated, but its source is a processed human vocal recording.
- The downloaded `Insect wings` paper-Foley and `Wings of insects` noise-generator source were excluded from the final previews.
- `previews/washer-pixabay-preview.wav` is wings, gated wing stridulation, and a wet organic impact.
- `previews/drone-bite-pixabay-preview.wav` is a short wing pickup, mandible click, and brittle crunch.
