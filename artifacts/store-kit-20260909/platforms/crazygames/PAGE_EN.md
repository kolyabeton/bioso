## Store copy

- Title: `BIOSO`
- One-line pitch: `Rebuild your biomech, shape a deadly loadout, and survive swarms beneath colossal machines.`
- Genre: action / survival / roguelite
- Tags: survival, robots, upgrades, bosses, science fiction

BIOSO is an action roguelite where every salvaged part changes how your creature fights. Move through a reclaimed machine world, assemble a build from interchangeable body parts and weapons, combine abilities into new synergies, and survive increasingly dangerous enemy swarms.

Weapons fire automatically. Your job is to move, dodge, choose upgrades, rebuild between threats, and prepare for colossal bosses that can reshape the battlefield.

## How to play

- Move: WASD, arrow keys, or drag with a mouse or finger.
- Assembly: I or the on-screen Assembly button.
- Map: M or the on-screen Map button.
- Pause: Escape or the on-screen Pause button.
- Weapons attack automatically.
