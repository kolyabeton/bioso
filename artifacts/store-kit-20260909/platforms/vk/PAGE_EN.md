BIOSO is an action roguelite about survival and rebuilding your own creature. Collect new body parts, create unusual combinations of weapons and abilities, cut a path through machine swarms, and prepare for colossal boss encounters.

Weapons attack automatically. Move, dodge, choose upgrades, and reshape your build as the run evolves.
