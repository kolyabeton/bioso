## Vertical: Scrap Leviathan swarm

Use case: ads-marketing. Vertical 9:16 promotional key art master for BIOSO
store pages and a vertical video cover. Create an exceptionally dynamic mass
battle in which a small assembled biomechanical player creature confronts a
colossal segmented boss while a dense swarm closes in from all sides.

Use `biomecha-style-master.png` only for authoritative materials, palette,
lighting, atmosphere, cinematic realism, and weathering; do not copy its robot
or composition. Preserve the Scrap Leviathan reference as an enormous long
centipede-like machine with dirty warm-white ceramic armor, black industrial
joints and claws, and restrained cyan nodes. Preserve the small modular BIOSO
creature's visual language and scale.

Set the battle in overgrown high-altitude industrial gardens made from
fractured stone terraces, ruined white ceramic infrastructure, dark wreckage,
desaturated olive vegetation, clouds and sparse wind technology. The boss
dominates the upper two-thirds and twists through the terraces. The hero fires
into an exposed segment from the lower center while dozens of varied small
biomechanical enemies surge through readable curved lanes. Use dust, debris,
sparks, cyan projectiles and a few warm impacts without hiding silhouettes.

Use a dramatic elevated three-quarter view close to the game's overhead
projection. Keep a clear scale contrast and focal hierarchy, with modest dark
breathing room near the top for an optional separately composited logo. No
text, logo, UI, watermark, border, cartoon rendering, painterly brushwork, toy
diorama, glossy plastic, ornate fantasy armor, saturated foliage, heavy sepia,
generic humanoid robots, empty space, excessive explosions, or gore.
