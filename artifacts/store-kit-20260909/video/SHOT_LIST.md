# Dynamic store video plan

The current clips are development evidence, not a finished store trailer. Record
the following beats from the final production build, without debug UI, capture
controls, browser chrome, or staged features absent from the public build.

## Shared gameplay sequence

1. `00:00-00:02` — open on the selected key art, then cut directly into motion.
2. `00:02-00:06` — a dense survival wave; the player crosses through enemies while two weapon systems fire.
3. `00:06-00:09` — rapid assembly choice: replace one visible part and return to combat.
4. `00:09-00:13` — a distinctive ability combination activates and clears a readable lane through the swarm.
5. `00:13-00:18` — colossal boss attack, clear telegraph, last-second dodge and counterattack.
6. `00:18-00:22` — second boss or mission environment for variety.
7. `00:22-00:25` — strongest impact frame, then BIOSO logo for channels that allow an end card.

## Platform cuts

- `crazygames-landscape-16x9.mp4`: 15-20 seconds, silent, start from the matching cover, no logo transition, promotional text, cursor, social/store icon, or black bars.
- `crazygames-portrait-2x3.mp4`: same edit reframed to 2:3, silent.
- `yandex-vertical-9x16-ru.mp4` and `yandex-vertical-9x16-en.mp4`: up to 28 seconds, gameplay-led and localized.
- `yandex-horizontal-16x9-ru.mp4` and `yandex-horizontal-16x9-en.mp4`: up to 28 seconds, gameplay-led and localized.
- `telegram-preview-9x16-ru.mp4` and `telegram-preview-9x16-en.mp4`: short localized preview optimized for the bot profile.
- `vk-trailer-16x9-ru.mp4` and `vk-trailer-16x9-en.mp4`: page trailers based on the same honest gameplay sequence.

The generated key art may be used as a brief opener, cover, or storyboard frame.
It must not replace real gameplay for most of a store video.
