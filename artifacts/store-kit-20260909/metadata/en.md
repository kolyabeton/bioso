# BIOSO — EN materials

## Title

BIOSO

## Short description

Build a biomechanical creature from scavenged parts, survive enemy swarms, and challenge colossal bosses.

## Description

BIOSO is an action roguelite about survival and rebuilding your own creature. Explore a world where nature is reclaiming forgotten technology, collect new body parts, and shape a build around the way you fight.

Your weapons attack automatically. Focus on movement, choose upgrades, dodge dangerous attacks, and decide which parts are worth carrying into the next battle.

- Assemble your creature from interchangeable bodies, weapons, legs, and internal organs.
- Discover abilities and combine upgrades into new synergies.
- Fight enemy swarms, elite creatures, and colossal bosses.
- Take on survival runs and handcrafted missions.

The game is in development. Content and balance may change.

## Controls

- Move: WASD, arrow keys, or drag across the play field with a mouse or finger.
- Assembly: I or the on-screen Assembly button.
- Map: M or the on-screen Map button.
- Pause: Escape or the on-screen Pause button.
- Weapons attack automatically.
